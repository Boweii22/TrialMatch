How It Works

Four steps.
One verdict.

No accounts, no configuration, no medical knowledge required. Upload your records and let Gemma do the rest.

Step 01
📄

Upload Your Records

Drop any PDF — discharge summary, lab report, clinical notes. PyMuPDF extracts text directly. Everything stays local.

Step 02
🎯

Pick Condition & Language

Select your primary condition and preferred language. TrialMatch fetches live open trials from ClinicalTrials.gov in real time.

Step 03
🧠

Gemma Reads & Matches

Gemma 4 extracts your full medical profile — diagnoses, medications, labs — then cross-references eligibility criteria for every trial.

Step 04
✉️

Verdict + Email Drafted

MATCH, PARTIAL, or NO — with a full reason, reasoning chain, and a professional email to the real trial coordinator ready to send.

Features

Built different.
Built for patients.

Everything a hospital system would build — running entirely on your CPU, in your living room, in your language.

🔒

100% Local Inference

Gemma 4 E4B runs via Ollama on your CPU. Your medical records are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored outside your machine. Verifiable — the model runs on your hardware.

Zero cloud
🎯

Smart Eligibility Matching

Extracts diagnosis, medications, lab values, allergies, and exclusion flags — then cross-references them against real trial criteria to deliver a precise MATCH / PARTIAL / NO verdict.

90% accuracy
📡

Live ClinicalTrials.gov Data

Pulls real, currently open trials from the official NIH database. Falls back to local DB when offline. You always see current, accurate trials — never stale cached data.

Real trials
🧠

Clinical Reasoning Chain

For every MATCH or PARTIAL verdict, Gemma generates a step-by-step clinical explanation — which criteria were checked, which values matched, and why the verdict was reached.

Transparent AI
✉️

Auto-Drafted Coordinator Email

One click generates a professional inquiry email addressed to the real coordinator of the real trial, using your de-identified medical profile. Hospital-grade language, instantly.

1-click email
🌍

9 Languages

Full results translated into Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Spanish, French, Simplified Chinese, Swahili, Portuguese, and Bengali. Because your language should never be a barrier to your healthcare.

Privacy First

Your data never leaves this room.

No cloud API. No third-party model. No telemetry. No account creation. TrialMatch is the only AI health tool where you can genuinely verify that claim — because the model runs on your hardware, not ours.

Medical records are read locally by PyMuPDF. Gemma 4 processes everything on-device. ClinicalTrials.gov is queried by condition name only — never by patient data.
